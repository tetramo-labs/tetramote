Privacy policy

Last updated November 20, 2026. Tetramote is made by Tetramo, Inc.

What we collect

Nothing. Tetramote has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting, and Tetramo runs no server that sees your sessions.

How a session travels

Your phone, browser or computer connects straight to the computer you control with iroh. The screen, sound, clipboard and your input are end to end encrypted between the two devices. When a direct connection isn't possible, the encrypted traffic passes through a relay: n0's public relays by default, or one you set. A relay forwards the encrypted packets and sees the two devices' IP addresses, but it can't read the session.

What stays on your devices

  • Each device's key and the list of devices it is paired with, kept in the system keychain or the app's settings.
  • Your settings, such as the relay address and trackpad speed.

Pairing links carry their secret after the #, which browsers never send to a web server. Remove a device on the computer and it can no longer connect.

Permissions

  • Camera: only to scan the pairing code your computer shows. The picture isn't saved or sent anywhere.
  • Local network: to reach your computer directly when both are on the same network.
  • On the computer, Screen Recording and Accessibility: to share its screen and take input from devices you paired.

Websites and updates

tetramote.com and remote.tetramote.com are static pages served by Cloudflare, which keeps standard request logs. The desktop app checks GitHub for updates. Neither is sent anything about your sessions.
